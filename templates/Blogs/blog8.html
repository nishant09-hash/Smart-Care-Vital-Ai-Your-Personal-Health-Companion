Cardio Training: Benefits and Myths

By Coach Anita | August 25, 2025

Cardio improves heart health, endurance, blood circulation, and metabolic efficiency. It is one of the most effective tools for overall wellness.

A common myth is that cardio destroys muscle. In reality, balanced cardio with good nutrition and strength training supports body composition goals.

You do not need long sessions daily. Even 20 to 30 minutes of brisk walking, cycling, or interval training can produce meaningful benefits.
