Importance of Sleep for Recovery

By Dr. Ramesh | August 28, 2025

Sleep is not just rest, it is your body's repair window. During deep sleep, muscles recover, hormones rebalance, and your brain consolidates learning.

If you train hard but sleep poorly, recovery slows down and injury risk goes up. Most adults should aim for 7 to 9 hours of quality sleep each night.

Simple improvements like consistent sleep timing, lighter late-night meals, and less screen exposure before bed can dramatically improve recovery outcomes.
