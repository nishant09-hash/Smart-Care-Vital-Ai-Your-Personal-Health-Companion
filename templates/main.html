What is BMI?

"Body Mass Index (BMI) is a Calculation Based on Height and Weight that helps 
    identify Whether a person is underweight, healthy, overweight, 
    or obese. While it doesn’t directly measure body fat, it is a 
    simple and widely used indicator of overall health."
